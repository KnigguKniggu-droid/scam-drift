SCAM DRIFT | TWO-MINUTE Q&A

Kartikeya: model, data, evaluation. William: frontend, UX, live demo.
Answer in about 20 seconds. Give one finding and, when relevant, its limit.

WHY THIS PROJECT?  A bank impersonation text asks for a code. The user needs to know what to do before replying. Scam Drift gives a warning, a reason, and a next action. GREEN leaves the sender unverified.

MAIN RESULT?  The 2012 model caught 75.65% of 1,080 real 2022–24 scam reports. Retraining caught 96.57% but flagged 597 of 600 genuine texts. Adding modern genuine training examples brought that false-alarm rate to 71.17% and kept recall at 95.93%. The genuine test cohort came from one inbox.

IS IT DRIFT?  The two sources differ. A source classifier had 0.9893 ROC AUC, so the gap mixes time and collection effects. We need consistently collected data across years to isolate time.

WHAT DID YOU TRAIN?  Submitted study: word TF-IDF, logistic regression, normalized links and numbers. Current demo: character n-gram model, fine-tuned English BERT-Tiny on CPU, and request and safety rules. BERT was added after submission.

WHAT CAN SLIP THROUGH?  A friendly opener may come before the money request. We also reproduced a miss on a portfolio sign-off text when its visible URL was absent. The checker sees only the pasted text.

HOW DO YOU KNOW IT GENERALIZES?  We split dated reports by time, removed exact duplicates, and checked near-template overlap. The one-inbox genuine cohort and source differences limit generalization. We cannot claim population accuracy.

WHAT NEXT?  Collect consented, redacted legitimate texts from many users. Hold out entire senders and campaigns. Test whether older adults understand and act on the warnings.

IF ASKED ABOUT LIVE MODEL NUMBERS  Full current web policy warned on 611 of 927 English scam reports from unseen templates and on 173 of 608 held-out genuine messages. That is a different evaluation from the submitted study. The eight-worker run completed 512 of 512 requests, which measures operation rather than detection quality.

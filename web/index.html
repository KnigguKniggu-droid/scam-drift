Check a text before you pay, tap or reply.

Got a text about your bank, a delivery or a payment? Paste it here. The checker warns you; it never blocks a message and it never says a text is safe.

    What the answers mean

    • High risk

      Looks like reported scams and has a link or a phone number.

    • Be careful

      Looks like a scam with no link, or asks for a code, or starts like a “new number” text. Ask family first.

    • No warning signs

      The checker found nothing. This does not prove the text is safe.

    The checker misses some scams. Never share a code, PIN or password, whatever the answer.

    What this does not prove.

    • The three answers, their rules and the strict warning level were designed after we saw the first results. Treat the shipped numbers as optimistic.
    • It mainly reads English. It warns on – of real non-English scams as written, and on – when we score the dataset's own English translation (a best case, not a feature of this app).
    • Some test texts are synthetic: written with AI help for this project and labelled SYNTHETIC everywhere. The checker warns on – of the synthetic legitimate texts it never trained on.
    • The checker never says a text is safe. If a text asks you to pay, tap or reply, contact the company yourself on a number you already trust.
